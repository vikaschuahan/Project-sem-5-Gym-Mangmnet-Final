Problem Statement

Why a Gym Management System?

📋

Manual Records

Gyms still rely on paper registers and spreadsheets, leading to errors and data loss.

⏳

Slow Operations

Manual follow-ups on memberships and enquiries waste staff time every single day.

📉

No Insights

No real-time view of active members, revenue, or equipment condition at a glance.

Solution → A centralized digital management platform
Core Modules

What the System Does

👥

Member Management

Register members with full profiles — DOB, gender, contact, emergency info, medical conditions, membership plan & join/expiry dates.

🏋️

Equipment Tracking

Maintain inventory with category, condition, quantity, purchase date & cost. Know what needs servicing at a glance.

💳

Membership Plans

Create flexible plans with duration, pricing & features. Members are linked to a plan with automatic expiry calculation.

📩

Enquiry Management

Track walk-in leads with branch, enquiry type, preferred callback date, status (Pending/Converted) & additional notes.

📅

Attendance System

Mark daily attendance (Present/Absent) per member with unique date constraint — one record per member per day.

🧑‍🏫

Trainer Management

Record trainer details, shift (Morning/Evening/Full), specialty (Yoga/CrossFit/Cardio/etc.) and salary.

Data Layer

Database Models

  • contact, email

  • medical_conditions

  • initialamount

🏋️ Equipment

  • name, category
  • quantity, price, unit
  • date (purchase)
  • condition (new/old)
  • description
